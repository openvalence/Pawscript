# Icons

`<Icon name size={16} />`, names from `ICON_NAMES` (icons.js). 16 in controls, 14 in `sm`; the glyphs are
drawn on the 16 grid, so 16 and 32 are the crisp sizes. Drawing rules are at the top of icons.js; the review
sheet is `dev/kit.html?demo=icons` (`&mode=grid` for the pixel grid and the true 1x/2x rasters).
Aliases share one glyph: `cut`/`scissors`, `delete`/`trash`, `add`/`plus`, `remove`/`minus`, `close`/`x`,
`settings`/`sliders`, `grip`/`drag`.

**Transport** — `play` · `pause` · `stop` · `record` · `step-back`/`step-forward` (one frame) ·
`skip-back`/`skip-forward` (to start/end, previous/next marker) · `rewind`/`fast-forward` · `loop` (loop
playback) · `speed` (playback rate) · `volume` · `mute` · `play-circle`/`stop-circle` (start/stop a job)

**Edit** — `undo` · `redo` · `cut`/`scissors` · `copy` · `paste` · `add`/`plus` · `remove`/`minus` ·
`delete`/`trash` · `duplicate` · `pen` (draw points) · `pointer` (select tool) · `select` (marquee
selection) · `move` · `hand` (pan) · `magnet` / `magnet-off` (snapping on/off) · `snap` (snap to guide) ·
`split` (razor: cut a clip in two) · `flip` (invert vertically) · `flip-h` (mirror in time) · `wand`
(auto/transform) · `bolt` (quick action) · `brush` · `eraser` · `cursor-text`

**Script and motion** — `wave` (generator) · `stroke` (stroke pattern) · `smooth` · `simplify` ·
`normalize` (fit to the range) · `repeat-stroke` · `random` · `axis` · `keyframe` · `bezier` (curve) ·
`heart-rate` (pulse/activity) · `ghost` (onion skin / ghost track)

**View** — `zoom-in` · `zoom-out` · `zoom-fit` (fit width) · `eye` · `eye-off` · `lock` · `unlock` ·
`maximize` (window) · `fullscreen` · `exit-fullscreen` · `expand` · `collapse` · `pip` (picture in
picture) · `panel-left` · `panel-right` · `panel-bottom` · `layers` · `grid` · `list` · `frame` · `sun` ·
`moon` · `contrast`

**Timeline** — `timeline` · `chapters` · `bookmark` · `flag` · `marker` · `loop-in` / `loop-out` (loop
region start/end) · `heatmap` · `ruler` · `clock` · `timer` (hourglass) · `stopwatch` · `history` · `pin`

**Tracking and devices** — `target` (follow) · `roi` (region of interest) · `crosshair` (track point) ·
`film` (video) · `image` (frame grab) · `device` · `machine` (toy on a carriage) · `plug` · `link` · `unlink` · `bluetooth` · `wifi` · `usb`
· `battery` · `power` · `cpu` · `gpu`

**Status** — `info` · `warning` · `error` · `success` · `help` · `check` · `dot` · `bell` · `bug` · `log` ·

**Files** — `file` · `folder` · `folder-open` · `save` · `export` (write out) · `import` (read in) ·

**Chrome** — `settings`/`sliders` · `tune` · `gear` · `search` · `filter` · `close`/`x` · `menu` · `more` ·
`more-v` · `grip`/`drag` · `grip-h` · `chevron-down` · `chevron-up` · `chevron-left` · `chevron-right` ·
`chevrons-up-down` · `arrow-up` · `arrow-down` · `arrow-left` · `arrow-right` · `keyboard` · `command`
